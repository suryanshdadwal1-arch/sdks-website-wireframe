Information needed from the school

We have the discovery workbook and the principal’s website draft. The requests below cover the remaining information and confirmations needed to complete the website content. Items not relevant to the school can be marked “Not applicable”.

What we already have

  • School name and location: SDKS Sri Sri Academy, Patiala. Nursery–Class V is the first-year range in the discovery and current brief.
  • About, vision and mission; play-based foundational learning and inquiry-based learning for Classes III–V.
  • Lists of planned facilities, sports, cultural activities, houses, clubs, life skills, assemblies and meditation.
  • Requested website areas: admissions, prospectus, uniforms, scholarship information, contact and careers.
  • Latest design feedback: the headline “The Art of Living. The Joy of Learning.” from reference image 2, with a homepage body arranged like image 1.

These details support the draft website. The school still needs to approve the edited wording; the reference images do not establish facts about facilities, fees, enrolment, dates or affiliation.

1. Confirm first

School identity and affiliation

Used on About, header/footer and Disclosure

Please confirm the exact school name, legal/trust names and the wording permitted for the Sri Sri / Art of Living association. Please also confirm the current CBSE affiliation status and share applicable supporting documents. The principal’s draft says “To be affiliated to CBSE”; we have not changed this to an affiliation claim.

Classes and dates

Used on Learning and Admissions

Please confirm whether entry starts at Nursery or Pre-Nursery—the discovery and principal’s draft differ. Share the exact early-years class names, eligibility/age criteria, intended academic session and admission opening date when decided. Confirm what may be said about expansion beyond Class V.

Official contact and visitor location

Used on Contact, enquiries and Maps

Please share the official phone, WhatsApp, email, office hours and admission-enquiry contact. Confirm the campus visitor address and Google Maps pin. The address approved for invoicing must not be assumed to be the visitor entrance.

Campus readiness

Used on Home, Campus, Sports and Gallery

For each listed facility and sport, mark: ready, under development, or planned for later. Share a dated construction update and current photographs or approved architectural renders. Confirm the track specification, usable outdoor areas and age suitability of sports. We have not used the acreage, student counts, staffing numbers or completion percentages shown in the design references.

2. Complete the page content

Principal and management

About

Names, roles, short biographies, relevant qualifications, approved portraits and a principal’s message of approximately 150–200 words. No need to rewrite the vision and mission already provided—please review our edited version instead.

Teaching and everyday learning

Learning, Nursery–II and Classes III–V

Teaching languages and subject details; two or three real examples of planned learning activities for each stage; the approach to assessment, parent communication and learning support. Confirm school timings, class sizes and what the lower/higher-class section counts in discovery refer to.

Care, safety and transport

Stage pages, Campus and Admissions

Confirmed supervision, visitor access and pickup arrangements; first aid/medical support; settling-in and toilet assistance for younger children; accessibility provisions. If transport is offered, share availability, routes, supervision and charges. Describe the actual arrangements rather than general assurances.

Sports, activities and values

Sports & Wellbeing and School Life

Which activities will run at launch, for which ages and with what supervision? Share short descriptions of houses, clubs, cultural activities, life skills, yoga and meditation. Confirm whether community/service activities are planned; these are not assumed from the visual reference.

Admission process and fees

Admissions and Fees & Information

Approved process, eligibility, required documents, enquiry/application route, class-wise fees, payment schedule, inclusions and additional charges. Share the prospectus and uniform guidelines. Confirm whether scholarships are offered; if so, provide the eligibility and terms.

Careers and updates

Careers and School Updates

Share genuine vacancies, qualifications, deadlines and the official application route, if recruitment is active. Supply any approved school notices with publication dates. Empty sections can stay out of the live navigation until needed.

3. Documents and media before launch

Disclosure information

Mandatory Public Disclosure

Please have the school’s responsible person check the applicable disclosure list and provide the documents: affiliation/recognition and trust registration; NOC where applicable; building/fire safety; DEO/self-certification where applicable; water, health and sanitation documents; fee schedule, academic calendar and committee information; staff qualifications and infrastructure details. Mark genuinely inapplicable items accordingly. This checklist is not certification of compliance.

Approved images and identity assets

Across the website

Final approved logo files and identity guide; principal/management portraits; dated campus photographs; labelled renders; any authorised learning/activity images. Confirm image permissions and any required consent. Do not present stock or staged images as actual enrolled pupils or completed facilities.

Enquiry handling and privacy

Forms, Contact and Privacy

Who receives enquiries and job applications? Confirm the approved collection fields, storage/access arrangements, retention practice and privacy contact. We will reflect the actual implementation in the privacy notice. The review forms currently send and store nothing.

How to send the material

Please organise the response under: School details · People · Academics · Campus & safety · Admissions · Documents · Photos. For each item, share the file or approved wording, or mark it “To follow” / “Not applicable”. Nominate one person to consolidate the school’s factual and copy approvals.
