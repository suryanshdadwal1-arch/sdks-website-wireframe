What to send for the website

We have used the completed discovery responses and the principal’s website draft. There is no need to resend the school introduction, vision, mission or programme lists.

Please share these documents

Please send clear PDF copies of the documents below in the existing school group or share a Google Drive folder link with our team. We will place the approved files on the website’s Mandatory Public Disclosure page. You do not need to upload them on this checklist page.

If a document is not ready, write “Pending”. If it does not apply, write “Not applicable” with a short reason. Please do not send student records or identity documents.

Certificates and approvals

  1. Society / trust registration certificate, with renewal if applicable.
  2. School recognition certificate under the RTE Act, with renewal if applicable.
  3. State government NOC (No Objection Certificate), if applicable.
  4. Building safety certificate — current and valid.
  5. Fire safety certificate — current and valid.
  6. Drinking water, health and sanitation certificates — current and valid.
  7. DEO certificate or school self-certification used for affiliation, as applicable.
  8. CBSE affiliation letter, including any extension or upgradation, only if issued. Otherwise, confirm the current status.

Please provide complete, readable copies, with the required school attestation by the principal and chairman / manager / secretary. The school should confirm which documents apply at its current stage. We will retain “To be affiliated to CBSE” unless the school supplies an updated approval.

School information for the same page

  • Final fee structure — class-wise fees and inclusions.
  • Academic calendar — for the first session.
  • School Management Committee (SMC) list.
  • Parent–Teacher Association (PTA) list, if formed; otherwise confirm its status.
  • Approved staff and infrastructure details — principal’s qualification, teaching staff, classrooms, labs, campus area and other disclosure particulars. A Word or Excel file is fine.
